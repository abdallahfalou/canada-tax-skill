## Which Forms Does This Taxpayer Need?

Use this decision tree:

**Always file:** T1, ON428

**If employed:** Check T4 for employment income, CPP/EI contributions, union dues. If working from home → T2200 + T777

**If made donations:** Schedule 9 + ON-BEN

**If have investments:** Schedule 3 (if sold), T2209 (if foreign tax paid)

**If have RRSP:** Schedule 7

**If have FHSA:** Schedule 15

**If have children:** T778 (childcare), Schedule ON479-A (CARE credit)

**If paid rent or property tax:** ON-BEN (for OEPTC)

**If self-employed:** Schedule 8 (CPP), Schedule 13 (EI), T2125

**If have foreign property > $100K:** T1135

**If claimed employment expenses:** Form GST370 (for HST rebate on those expenses)
