## Ontario Surtax

### 2024
- 20% of basic ON tax over $5,554
- 36% of basic ON tax over $7,108 (additional)
- Formula: `surtax = max(0, 0.20 * (tax - 5554)) + max(0, 0.36 * (tax - 7108))`

### 2025
- 20% of basic ON tax over $5,710
- 36% of basic ON tax over $7,307 (additional)

## Ontario Tax Reduction (ON428 Lines 74-80)

Eligibility: Canadian resident at year start, Ontario resident Dec 31, not subject to minimum tax, not bankrupt.

| Component | 2024 | 2025 |
|---|---|---|
| Basic reduction | $286 | $294 |
| Per dependent child | $529 | $544 |
| Per disabled dependant | $529 | $544 |

Total is doubled, then subtracted from Ontario tax. Higher-income spouse claims children.

## LIFT Credit (Schedule ON428-A)

Low-Income Individuals and Families Tax credit. For Ontario residents with employment income. Reduces Ontario tax for low-income workers. Calculated on Schedule ON428-A.

---

## Community Food Program Donation Credit

For Ontario farmers donating agricultural products to community food programs. 25% of qualifying donations. Products must be Ontario-produced, minimally processed. 5-year carryforward.

## Ontario Refundable Credits (Form ON479) — Full Details

### 1. Fertility Treatment Tax Credit (NEW for 2025)
- 25% of eligible expenses up to $20,000 = max $5,000
- Eligible: medical expenses for conception treatment, surrogate/donor expenses (services entirely in Canada)
- Only one spouse claims
- Can combine with federal/Ontario medical credits for same expenses

### 2. CARE Tax Credit (Childcare)
- For parents claiming child care on federal line 21400
- Income-tested via Schedule ON479-A
- Not available if bankrupt

### 3. Seniors Care at Home Tax Credit
- Age 70+ (you or spouse) on Dec 31
- Medical expenses from ON428 line 58769 x 25%, max $1,500
- Clawback: 5% of family income over $35,000
- Zero if family income > $65,000

### 4. Seniors' Public Transit Tax Credit
- Age 65+ on Dec 31
- 15% of qualifying transit payments up to $3,000 = max $450
- Eligible: bus, subway, train, tram (Ontario municipal transit)
- Must use senior fare if available

### 5. Political Contribution Tax Credit
- Ontario political party/constituency/candidate contributions
- Graduated rate, max credit $1,666.82
- If contributions >= $3,793, credit = $1,666.82

### 6. Focused Flow-Through Share Tax Credit
- 5% of qualifying mining exploration expenses from Form T1221

### 7. Co-operative Education Tax Credit
- Unincorporated businesses hiring co-op students
- Max $3,000 per student per placement
- 30% rate if prior-year payroll <= $400,000; 25% if >= $600,000

## Ontario Trillium Benefit (Form ON-BEN) — Full Details

### Ontario Sales Tax Credit (OSTC)
- Automatic — CRA determines eligibility from return
- For age 19+ (or with spouse/child), low-to-moderate income

### Ontario Energy and Property Tax Credit (OEPTC)
- Tick box 61020
- Qualifies if: paid rent or property tax for principal residence, or lived in LTC, or paid energy costs on reserve
- Lines: rent (61100), property tax (61120), student residence (61140), reserve energy (61210), LTC (61230)
- Part B: address, postal code, months resident, amount paid, landlord/municipality name
- Only one spouse applies; if one is 64+, they must apply

### Northern Ontario Energy Credit (NOEC)
- Tick box 61040
- Northern Ontario districts only: Algoma, Cochrane, Kenora, Manitoulin, Nipissing, Parry Sound, Rainy River, Sudbury, Thunder Bay, Timiskaming

### Senior Homeowners' Property Tax Grant (OSHPTG)
- Tick box 61070
- Age 64+ on Dec 31, owned + occupied Ontario home, paid property tax
- Payment within 4-8 weeks of NOA

### Options
- Delayed payment: tick 61060 to receive full OTB as single payment instead of monthly
- Involuntary separation: tick 61080 if spouses living apart for medical reasons
