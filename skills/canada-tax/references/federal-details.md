### Carrying Charges (Line 22100) — Detail

**Eligible:**
- Investment management fees (not in RRSP/TFSA/FHSA)
- Investment advice fees
- Accounting fees for investment income
- Interest on borrowed money to earn investment income
- Legal fees for support payments

**NOT eligible:**
- Safety deposit box fees
- Brokerage commissions (capital gains adjustment)
- Student loan interest (use line 31900)
- RRSP/TFSA/FHSA/RESP account fees
- Interest where only possible return is capital gains

### Child Care Expenses (Line 21400) — Detail

- Generally claimed by lower-income spouse
- Limits: $8,000 (under 7), $5,000 (7-16 or 16+ with infirmity), $11,000 (DTC-eligible)
- 2/3 rule: max = 2/3 of lower-income earner's net income
- Eligible: daycare, babysitters, day camps, boarding school (limited)
- NOT eligible: clothing, transportation, medical/hospital
- Form T778

### Moving Expenses (Line 21900) — Detail

- New home must be 40+ km closer to new work/school by shortest public route
- Eligible: packing/hauling, travel, temp living (max 15 days), lease cancellation, utility hookup, maintaining old home (max $5,000), legal costs, real estate commissions, mortgage penalties
- NOT eligible: renovations, house-hunting, job-hunting, cleaning, mail forwarding
- Carryforward allowed; Form T1-M

### Canada Workers Benefit — Detail

- 27% phase-in on working income over $3,000
- Phase-out: ~$23,495 (single) / ~$26,805 (family) for 2024
- Max: ~$1,518 (single), ~$2,616 (family)
- Disability supplement available
- Schedule 6

### Canada Training Credit — Detail

- Age 26-65, resident all year, with CTCL balance
- CTCL accumulation: $250/year if income between ~$10,000 and ~$150,000
- Claim: lesser of 50% of eligible tuition or available CTCL
- Each claim reduces future CTCL balance

### FHSA — Detail

- Annual contribution: $8,000
- Lifetime: $40,000
- Deductible like RRSP; Schedule 15
- Withdrawals tax-free for qualifying first home purchase
- Unused room carries forward (max $8,000/year)
- RRSP transfers to FHSA: not deductible

### RRSP — Detail

- 2024 limit: 18% of 2023 earned income, max $31,560 + unused room
- 2025 limit: 18% of 2024 earned income, max $32,490 + unused room
- Home Buyers' Plan: withdraw up to $60,000 tax-free, repay over 15 years
- Lifelong Learning Plan: $10,000/year ($20,000 total), repay over 10 years
